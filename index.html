Chapter One

Games, Media & the Industry

For over a decade, games have been the thread running through Ben's career — first as a writer and critic, then as a media producer, and eventually as a publishing professional helping indie studios get their work in front of the world.

2025 – 2026

indie.io

As Marketing Manager and Project Lead at indie.io, Ben published, marketed, and managed 75+ indie games in under 18 months — across PC, console, and mobile — within a 200+ title active portfolio that generated 3M+ Steam wishlist additions and 1.3M+ units sold during his tenure.

Beyond managing titles, Ben built a full suite of internal tools: Steamworks automation, creator outreach platforms, festival submission workflows, developer communications systems, and a press kit platform — turning what had been the most labor-intensive parts of the job into streamlined, repeatable processes.

2021 – 2025

Lillymo Games

As Producer and Production Manager, Ben managed platform submission workflows across PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, and Steam for Lillymo's catalog — handling metadata, certification, pricing, promotions, and release coordination. He worked directly with platform representatives to resolve issues, negotiate visibility, and keep releases moving through complex multi-platform pipelines.

Chapter Two

Mayor of Butler, Pennsylvania

In 2017, while running Handsome Phantom and Lambsmith PR, Ben ran for Mayor of Butler, Pennsylvania. He won with 60% of the vote and served a full term from 2018 to 2022.

"It's Smith in a Rout"

Butler Eagle, November 8, 2017

Running a City

For four years, Ben led all city operations for Butler — a municipality of roughly 15,000 residents — overseeing a $12M+ annual budget across public safety, infrastructure, code enforcement, and community development. One term was always the plan. He ran to get things done, not to make a career of it.

The role demanded the same skills that define everything else he has done: managing complex operations, coordinating across departments that don't always agree, communicating clearly with people who have real stakes in the outcome, and building systems that outlast any single person.

Chapter Three

Entrepreneur

Long before the games industry, Ben was building something in downtown Butler — a brewery that became a gathering place, and later a speakeasy that turned an old space into something people still talk about.

2013 – 2022

Reclamation Brewing Company

Ben co-founded Reclamation Brewing in Butler, PA and ran it for nine years alongside everything else — a staff of around 20, a full operational build-out from scratch, and the daily reality of running a small business in a mid-size city. Payroll, scheduling, vendor relations, marketing, community events, financial planning. All of it.

Reclamation became a real anchor for the community downtown — a place people came back to. Building that from nothing remains one of Ben's proudest achievements.

2013 – 2022

RB Speakeasy & Cigar Bar

Alongside the brewery, Ben and his partners built the RB Speakeasy — a cigar bar and cocktail lounge that brought something Butler didn't have before. Exposed brick, leather, craft cocktails, a walk-in humidor. The kind of place that takes real care to get right.
