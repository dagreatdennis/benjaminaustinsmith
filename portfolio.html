Product & Merchandise Portfolio

Hello!

I wanted to give you a quick look at some of the stuff I've worked on. Some of this I designed and built start to finish myself, other parts I product managed end to end — working across teams and with outside partners to get it out the door. Below is a mix of both, have a look!

Reclamation Brewing Company

Beyond running the day-to-day operations of the brewery, I helped design, produce, market, and sell everything from apparel to packaged beer. That meant working through the entire process, from initial concepts and vendor coordination to getting products on shelves and into customers' hands. We spent nearly a decade building Reclamation Brewing Co. before a fire irreparably damaged the building, forcing us to close our doors.

Licensing

Pixale

PIXALE began as a simple idea: officially licensed craft beers for video games. A single tweet turned into licensing agreements with publishers and developers across the industry, including Atlus, Devolver Digital, Raw Fury, Rivals of Aether, and Thunderful. Although COVID-19 halted our first launch at GDC 2020 and the project ultimately ended when a fire destroyed our brewery, the experience involved everything from contract negotiations and partner management to product development and licensing.

Last Stand Media

At Last Stand Media, I worked as a Producer while also owning much of the company's merchandise and live event operations. I sourced artists, coordinated with manufacturers, managed product timelines, built and maintained our Shopify storefront, and helped organize live events across the country. Merchandise releases regularly sold out, with some products selling through their entire preorder allocation before they even reached production.

Lillymo Games

As Lillymo Games' sole Producer, I helped ship five titles across console and PC while also overseeing several physical releases. That included coordinating disc production through Eastasiasoft, sourcing and producing printed pack-ins and other bonus materials, and managing the products through launch, including listing and selling them through the online store.

indie.io

At indie.io, I managed marketing and publishing efforts for more than 75 indie games, serving as the primary point of contact for roughly 35 developer partners at any given time. Every title required balancing timelines, budgets, creative assets, storefronts, events, and cross-functional teams to get games successfully to market. I also developed internal tools and workflows that streamlined operations across a publishing portfolio of more than 200 active games.
